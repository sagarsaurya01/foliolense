What’s in the App vs the Demo

A plain map of what works on real data today, and what the showcase demo adds as the vision. As of 28 Oct 2026.

In the app — works on your real data In the demo, not the app — shown in the showcase Not in app or demo — further vision

1 · Live in the app

real features, working on your own portfolio
Tracking & portfolio
  • ✓ All holdings in one place; net worth, invested, current value, total & today’s gain
  • ✓ Realised & unrealised gains; XIRR
  • ✓ Live daily prices from NSE & AMFI (matched by ISIN)
  • ✓ 12 asset classes: stocks/ETFs, equity & debt MF, SIF, banks, NPS/ULIP, insurance, private equity, FDs, deposits/loans, PPF/EPF, gold
  • ✓ Families; Individual / HUF / Company / Other portfolios & groups
  • ✓ Imports: tradebook, CAS, e-CAS, opening balances (Excel), manual entry
  • ✓ Corporate actions; analytics (allocation, XIRR performance, equity exposure)
Hindsight — “what happened to me” (new)
  • ✓ Did I make money — gain & %, booked gains this year with estimated tax (STCG 20%, LTCG 12.5% over the ₹1.25L 112A exemption, else slab)
  • ✓ 112A tax-harvest headroom
  • ✓ Top gainers & draggers; income this year (dividends + interest)
  • ✓ Your XIRR vs a bank FD and vs inflation
Review — “is my money arranged well” (new)
  • ✓ Allocation overview by broad group
  • ✓ Concentration (largest holding, top 5, trim amount)
  • ✓ Liquidity (how much is reachable in ~1 week)
  • ✓ Income (dividend/interest yield)
Reports / Tax pack
  • ✓ Realised capital gains (FIFO, short/long term, 31-Jan-2018 grandfathering)
  • ✓ Capital gains summary across all years; Schedule 112A export (CSV)
  • ✓ Dividends & interest report; holdings as on a date; transaction register
The demo shows all of the above — populated with a sample “Sharma family” covering every asset type — plus everything in section 2 below, so viewers see the complete product vision. The real app contains only section 1.
